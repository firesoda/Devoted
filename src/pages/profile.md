## Introduction

Yuzuru Fushimi is an eighteen-year old idol from the unit *fine* under Starmaker Production agency. Yuzuru also works as a butler for Tori Himemiya, a member of the same unit. As a butler, his job is to always be by his master's side. This is shown in his enrolment in Yumenosaki Private Academy where he signed himself up as an idol, together with Tori

Yuzuru has a short navy hair, a pair of fuschia eyes, and a beauty mark under his right eye. He is 175cm tall, slightly above the average height in Japan. Prior to joining fine, Yuzuru used to have a long ponytail hair and the look of a punk.

## Personality

![Yuzuru](../assets/graphics/chibi_yuzuru_01.png#left)

Yuzuru is known to be very calm and collected. He is polite and disciplined, as expected from a butler. Yuzuru is shown to have a caring personality and it's his wish to take care of others around him. Yuzuru speaks formally even when speaking to people his age and he finds it easier to speak that way as opposed to the casual speech. He primarily uses the personal pronoun 'watakushi', but switches to 'ore' or his own name when he gets too emotional.

Behind his motherly and nurturing personality hides his habit of making snarky comments and backhanded compliments which he often does around people close to him or around people he doesn't like. He's also revealed to possess a level of jealousy when someone gets too close to Tori, fearing that they would take his place.

## Life Before Idol

Yuzuru was Tori's butler before becoming an idol following his master's path, having worked for him since Yuzuru was a child! Yuzuru's family had been working for the Himemiya family for generations. He stated that his parents and grandparents worked as butlers before him, so that makes him the third generation of Fushimis to work for them as far as I know.

![Before becoming enroling as an idol, Yuzuru used to have a ponytail hair.](../assets/graphics/A_Distant_Days_Pain.jpg#right)

His parents had drilled the butler knowledge into him early in life, hoping that he continued to be Tori's lifelong companion. As a child, he was often scolded for his constant mistakes which caused him to be a crybaby. To correct this behaviour, Yuzuru was sent into a private military camp by his parentsand wasn't allowed to return until he had mastered the skills needed to be a proper butler, including the skills needed to protect Tori from danger. 

Yuzuru recalls his experience in the camp being rigorous. At such a young age, Yuzuru was trained on how to deal with abduction and assassination attempts. He hated that his parents subjected him to these kinds of training that he wished to 'dissolve' just to make his parents feel bad. Along with one other child, he was the youngest candidate in the camp.

Yuzuru still hated his family for what they did, yet as soon as he returned to the Himemiya mansion, Tori welcomed him full of warmth and love that it moved Yuzuru to the point of regaining his sense of purpose: to live on as Tori's butler.

This military experience has shaped how he reacted in times of danger. His 'odd' excitement shown during this time makes others scared or uncomfortable. Besides this, he's become slightly paranoid and overpepared for something he shouldn't have. This includes checking his surrounding for 'odd stuffs', such as traps and hidden cameras. He often has intrusive thoughts with one of the example being what would happen if he poured poison into the tea he was going to serve to his guests.
