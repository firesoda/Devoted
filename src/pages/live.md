## Introduction

Ensemble Stars originally started as an idol-themed management game. The IP soon expands into stage plays adaptation, later conducts concerts in which idols perform in a giant screen, and eventually holds concert where the idol's respective voice actors sing on stage.

## Dream Live, the virtual concert

Virtual concerts held by Ensemble Stars is called "Dream Live". Dream Lives are real-life concerts where characters are projected on a screen. While the idols' vocals are prerecorded, a live band is present to play the songs on stage. Dream Lives are usually held for a few months in selected venues, with lottery ticket system. At the venue, attendees can purchase and preorder various concert merchandise, from random badges to concert towels with idols printed on them.

For those who can't attend, DVDs or Blu-rays of the recorded Dream Live become available for purchase some time months after the event. The most recent live, Dream Live Tour 10th xALL STARS!!, was available online (through paid broadcast) and offline in theatres in Japan, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and South Korea.

![Promotional pictures of Yuzuru Fushimi for Dream Live concerts.](../assets/graphics/DL.png#center "Left to right: Dream Live 5th, 6th, 9th, and 10th Tour.")

Yuzuru, together with *fine*, first performed in Dream Live -5th Tour "Stargazer"- which was held between April-May 2021. *fine* would later make an appearance in Dream Live -6th Tour "Synchronic Spheres"- (May-June 2022), Dream Live -9th Tour "Trapezium #Orion"- (November-December 2024), and Dream Live Tour 10th xALL STARS!! (March-April 2026).

## Starry Stage and Starry Symphony

![Koutarou Hashimoto as Yuzuru in Starry Symphony -Superbloom-](../assets/graphics/Starry_Symphony_-Superbloom-_Koutaro_Hashimoto_1.jpg#left)

Starry Stage and Starry Symphony are concerts where instead of virtual idols, voice actors of respective idols take over the stage, dress up as the character they voice, and sing. Voice actors would often rehearse together and wear matching details on stage, such as dyeying part of their hair with their character's image colour.

Both Starry Stage and Starry Symphony are the same thing: real-life concert where voice casts perform on the stage, though both are managed by different organisers.

Koutarou Hashimoto (pictured) with others from *fine* first made an appearance in Day 2 of Starry Stage 4th "Star's Parade" (July and August 2021) as special guest. He later reprised his role in Starry Symphony -the midnight sun- (June 2024) and Starry Symphony -Superbloom- (November 2025).


## Ensemble Stage, the stage play adaptation

In 2016, Ensemble Stars started producing a series of stage plays called "Ensemble Stage!", also known as Enstage or Ansute. These stage plays adapts the game's main and event stories focused on one group or characters. The stage typically revolves around stories that are considered important to the characters or units portrayed. Over the years, Yuzuru has been portrayed by three stage actors.

![Yuzuru stage actors across the years. L-R: Yutaka Nozaki, Takaki Watanabe, Yuta Iiyama. Credit: Happy Elements](../assets/graphics/stage_actors.jpg#center "Left to right: Yutaka Nozaki, Takaki Watanabe, Yuta Iiyama. Credit: Happy Elements")

Yuzuru's first stage actor was Yutaka Nozaki from 2017 to 2018. He has appeared in three stage plays. He would disappear for some time until 2022 when he was spotted participating in Boys Planet as a contestant. After this, he briefly returned to stage acting before becoming the leader and one of the founding members of a Japanese boy group #Luvless.

Takaki Watanabe was Yuzuru's second stage actor. Similar to Nozaki, he played the role of Yuzuru for a year, from 2018 to 2019. Watanabe only appeared in two stage plays. At some point, he was a member of JUNON SUPERBOY ANOTHERS.

Yuta Iiyama has been the current stage actor of Yuzuru since 2022 and has starred in four stage plays. Before being Yuzuru's actor, he was an actor in stage plays of other medias, including Collar×Malice and i-Chu. During this time, he was a member of idol group B2takes and its sub-unit LUCK☆LIFE before graduating in 2022. Iiyama's specialty is archery and he has participated in tournaments in the past.

It's an interesting coincidence to learn that all Yuzuru stage actors were a boy group member at some point in their career!
