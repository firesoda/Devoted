## Introduction

Thanks for checking out **Devoted**! You can call me **Nev**. I've been playing Ensemble Stars Music since November 2021. My first oshi in the game was Izumi Sena, then I switched oshi to Adonis Otogari briefly before settling on Yuzuru Fushimi. "But who's Juis Kojika?" He's my side oshi (lol).

I no longer play the game on a regular basis. I only log in when I feel like it, or when Yuzuru makes an appearance in a current event or gacha banner. My interest kind of died down some time during the 10th anniversary of the game as they pushed heavy updates that rendered my phone slow. Despite this, I still buy merchandise I could find on marketplaces and participate in group orders. I believe that one doesn't need to constantly play the game in order to continue loving the character.

When it comes a type of fan, I lean towards being a silent fan. I can't draw, nor can I do cosplays (despite doing it twice) and write fan fictions, so I only exist in the space to everyone's hard work. I'm sort of a merch collector who now collects at their own pace after suffering from burnout and unemployment. 

## Why Yuzuru?

Yuzuru carries this aura of calmness with him. His dance moves are elegant. His voice soothes me, though sometimes I wish whoever arranges the music didn't make Yuzuru sing such high notes. My poor boy. 

What draws me in the most is his personality. He may be a poised man, but when danger closes in, he finds himself enjoying the thrill. He does this scary expression that makes other people uncomfortable. His thirst for blood gives a life to his character, making him not just a maid to others. This is supported by his abilities in combat, his experience in the military camp, and the years spent in archery club.

I see myself in Yuzuru's inability to have a purpose other than serving Tori. He becomes restless when he's not around the very person he cares the most, which creates an unhealthy attachment issue. To overcome this, Yuzuru picks up drawing and busies himself with cleaning and organising his surrounding. He's also opening himself to other people his age in hopes to not get overly attached to Tori, and he only did so because Tori ordered him so. Yuzuru teaches me that life is not at all devoting oneself to another person, but to find purpose in life that makes you feel alive.

Over the years, Yuzuru has become pretty much my comfort character. He's been there with me during the lowest points in my life. Going back to him feels like coming home after a long journey.

## Why this site?

**Devoted** was initially planned to be my personal site. At the time, I wanted to make a shrine page after feeling inspired to do so. What I thought to be a single page turned into five pages and with the scope this big, I decided to turn **Devoted** into a fan site instead.

I'm hoping to keep this site up for a long time as a reminder that I don't have to be scared of expressing my love for a fictional character. This stems from the fear of being judged in social media. I'm content to be doing all this without a 140-character limit!

## Do you ship Yuzuru with yourself? Do you yumeship? or-

I used to ship myself with Yuzuru, then I shipped him with my original character. Yes, I also shipped Yuzuru with other characters in the game. I don't ship a lot these days.
