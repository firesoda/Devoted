Welcome to **Devoted**, a fan site devoted to **Yuzuru Fushimi** from the Japanese mobile rhythm game **Ensemble Stars**. Yuzuru is an eighteen-year old idol of the unit *fine* (pronounced fee-neh) who also doubles as a butler to Tori Himemiya, a member of the same unit. An adaptable person, Yuzuru has proven himself to be a jack-of-all-trades in hopes to support his master and everyone he holds dear.

This site serves as a page where you can get to know Yuzuru, and a web shrine where I talk about being a Yuzuru fan! It's not intended to look professional like one would find in real fansites, so if you're expecting that level of professionalism, sorry this isn't the place! Most of the content you'll see are based on my interpretation, so please take it with a grain of salt~
