## Prior to Loving Yuzuru

Life was a shipwreck before Yuzuru came into my life. I was busy doing my thesis while dealing with the stress coming from the lockdown and a breakup from a long-standing relationship. I was barely functioning as a human, clouded with unsavoury thoughts  all the time. During this time, I desperately searched for escapism. I played countless otome and romance simulation games to get away from my own sadness, which led me to meeting a few amazing friends. One of them eventually discovered Ensemble Stars and streamed the gameplay to me, hoping I'd get into the game. It did work, sort of!

At first, I was drawn into the rhythm aspect of the game, but my opinion soon changed after watching idols dancing in the background while my friend was tapping notes on the screen. My first thought was: *woah, they've got 3D models!* I've played rhythm games before and most of them only have either plain backgrounds, static image, or videos... not 3D models dancing in the background. As someone who had never played idol-themed rhythm games, I thought that was very impressive.

![Yuzuru in Devoted Valet card, wearing butler outfit.](../assets/graphics/devotedvalet_bloomed.jpg#left)

I started playing Ensemble Stars on **2nd November 2021**. The game has so many characters from different units and with different personalities it was overwhelming at first. Before Yuzuru, I was a fan of a different character and was considering switching oshis after suffering from a high character tax. This specific character was (and still is) one of the most popular characters in the game. Coupled with the fact that this character comes from a unit that has a massive fan base, merchandise can be very hard to come by!

## Devoted Valet

On **24th November 2021**, I was on Twitter when suddenly I saw it. A new gacha banner featuring Yuzuru whom I didn't even know. Him in a gorgeous butler outfit. There was something undeniably attractive about that card (see left). Not sure what got into me at that time, but in that moment, I realised that I fell so hard for him. The card was translated by community to **Devoted Valet**, and in English version of the game, the card is called **Devoted Steward**. I personally stick to Devoted Valet as I'm so used to it.

I have to admit that the entire thing was corny and cheesy... whatever you call it. I suddenly found a motivation to keep applying for jobs, something to obsess on, and a reason to look forward to tomorrow and the days that come after. Like... "when you know, you know" kind of thing. Explaining to friends about this sudden attraction was so hard! How was I suppose to explain 'hey so this card really changed the trajectory of my life' when I don't even know which unit Yuzuru was from?!

When the card finally appeared in the game, I spent like 70 rolls but didn't get him! How cruel!! Though I should've known it was a gacha game whose rate was far worse than other gacha games I've played with pity system. That was the origin story of me breaking my free-to-play status through a 1000 yen bundle. Did I get him? No. Thanks to that terrible luck, I had my reason to play all the songs the game had to offer. All 100 of them and more. This resulted in my exclusive commitment to Yuzuru and only Yuzuru. Part of the reason was because I couldn't afford to pull for other characters.

![Yuzuru in Devoted Valet card, wearing butler outfit.](../assets/graphics/devotedvalet_scr.png#right)

In the end, it took me six months to finally have that card through a rerun, only to learn that there is a secret outfit that one can get after owning three copies and five copies of the same card. You can imagine my frustration when I found that out! Eventually, it took me another 2-3 years to finally have full copies of Devoted Valet so I could see Yuzuru wearing the white butler outfit in my home screen every time I open the game. This outfit is also available in the 3D music video, and I have fun taking screenshots of him in it!

## Was getting Devoted Valet my only goal?

![Yuzuru in Ghostic Phenomenon for Halloween 2022.](../assets/graphics/ghostic.jpg#left)

No! As I waited for another Devoted Valet rerun, I discovered that Yuzuru hadn't had his event yet. That event would happen on Halloween 2022, and the card was titled **Ghostic Phenomenon**. I was still unemployed, so I couldn't get more than one copy of the card. Regardless, I was happy to obtain one copy of this card as this was the first time I spent diamonds in an event, ever.

After that, I pretty much focus on collecting Yuzuru cards to complete my album. If I can't bring home maximum copies of his card, I'll aim for at least one. 

## It's been years. How come do you still like Yuzuru?

Yuzuru has been with me through my ups and downs. He was there when I graduanted and comforted me as I went through traumatic experiences. There was not a single day where I thanked Yuzuru for keeping me going.

To be honest with you, I wasn't always loyal to Yuzuru. I had moments where I got bored of him, played other games, and fell for other characters. Despite that, he was still in the back of my mind like he actually lived there. It feels like having an inside joke with your spouse about 'dating' other people. No matter how many people I obsess with, he has his own place in my heart and mind.

Another thing worth mentioning is that Yuzuru was the reason I was able to get out of my shell. Because of him, I started attending more anime cons, hoping I'd bump into other Enstars players. I signed myself up to fan gatherings in the city and I've made some friends there! Besides meeting other fans, I no longer cared about people calling me cringe when I went out with my itabag. I've always seen itabag as cringe until I actually went out with one. I was so, so wrong!

## Final note

My love for Yuzuru results in the birth of this site! **Devoted** was meant to have only one page, but I made the scope too big so now it sort of acts like a fan page. I feel bad knowing it might or might not have misconceptions, but I learn that not everything has to be perfect ♡
